# ASMS–ROUNAQH Design Direction

Adapted for ASMS–ROUNAQH from the warm flagship principles documented in VoltAgent's `awesome-design-md` Starbucks design analysis.

## Atmosphere

An established Chennai property and construction business: warm, grounded, premium and accountable. Editorial rather than marketplace-like. Avoid artificial urgency, unsupported social proof and overly glossy luxury cues.

## Core system

- Canvas: warm ivory `#F8F5EF`; lifted cards: white; secondary paper: `#EEE9DE`.
- Brand green: `#103F34`; dark green: `#092E27`; uplift green: `#285E50`.
- Antique gold `#AD792A` is ceremonial and used sparingly for high-priority actions and fine rules.
- Display typography: Cormorant Garamond, 54–80px desktop, 42–52px mobile.
- Body typography: Manrope, 14–18px, generous line height.
- Buttons are full pills; cards use restrained 16–20px radii and minimal shadows.
- Page rhythm: photographic/dark hero → ivory editorial content → white catalogue cards → deep-green evidence band → dark footer.
- Eyebrows use a small gold dot, uppercase letters and tracked spacing.

## Guardrails

- Use genuine business photographs when supplied; clearly label all temporary imagery and sample data.
- Present price, area, location and document status with equal visual weight.
- Use green as the trust anchor and gold only as an accent.
- Keep motion subtle and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Maintain 44px minimum touch targets and visible keyboard focus.
